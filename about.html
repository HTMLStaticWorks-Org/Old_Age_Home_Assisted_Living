Who we are

We see the person before the care plan.

Saanidhya is a senior living concept centred on warm hospitality, respectful support and partnership with families. We take time to understand each resident’s routines, relationships, interests and support needs.

Our team works with residents and families to shape an individual plan, review it as needs change and make sure everyone understands the next steps.

  • Choice and independence wherever possible
  • Privacy, respect and personal dignity
  • Clear communication and accountability
  • Safe, accessible and welcoming surroundings

Ongoing partnership

How we stay connected with families

Support works best when communication stays open before admission, during settling-in and over the long term.

01

Before move-in

Listen carefully to personal routines, health history and expectations so everyone begins with clarity.

02

During settling-in

Support gradual adjustment, welcome family visits and fine-tune daily assistance gently.

03

As needs evolve

Review the care plan periodically with the resident and family and agree clearly on next steps.

Our promise to families

Open conversations. Clear expectations.

We believe trust is built on plain, documented answers before anyone moves in. We encourage every family to ask questions and review terms carefully.

Speak with our team ↗

What should be shared in writing

  • Proposed individual care and daily support plan
  • Full fee schedule, inclusions and additional charges
  • Visiting arrangements and family communication norms
  • Resident rights, privacy terms and complaint process
