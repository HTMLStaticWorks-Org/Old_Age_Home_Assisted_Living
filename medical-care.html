Person-centred planning

Support begins with listening.

Care needs are different for every person. Before admission, a qualified professional should assess health history, mobility, medication, nutrition, cognition and daily support requirements. The resulting plan should be discussed with the resident and family and reviewed regularly.

  • Initial needs assessment and care planning
  • Medication coordination in line with approved procedures
  • Assistance with activities of daily living, where agreed
  • Physiotherapy and rehabilitation coordination, where available
  • Referrals and hospital coordination when clinically required

Important to know

Care transparency is essential.

Before choosing a residence, ask who provides clinical oversight, the staffing pattern, response arrangements, medication policy, hospital tie-ups, service exclusions and additional charges. Do not assume on-site doctors, 24/7 nursing, ICU access or specialist memory-care programmes unless these are confirmed in writing.
