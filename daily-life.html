A balanced rhythm

Days shaped around interests and abilities.

Activities are most meaningful when residents help shape them. The programme below is an example; actual sessions should reflect resident preferences, accessibility and staff availability.

  • Gentle movement, yoga or chair-based exercise
  • Gardening, nature walks and outdoor time
  • Music, bhajans and cultural celebrations
  • Arts, crafts, reading and shared games
  • Family visits and intergenerational moments
