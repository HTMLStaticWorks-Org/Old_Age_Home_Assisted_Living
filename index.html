Questions families ask

Clear answers before you decide.

Choosing an assisted living residence raises practical questions. Here are straightforward answers to help guide your conversation.

Ask a specific question →

Can family members visit before making a decision?

Yes. Visits are arranged by appointment so you can walk the campus, see room concepts and speak directly with the team.

How are individual care needs assessed?

Before admission, a qualified professional reviews health history, mobility, medication, nutrition and daily support needs with the resident and family.

What should families verify before confirming admission?

Always request a written explanation of the care plan, fee schedule, inclusions, visiting policy, resident rights and emergency escalation arrangements.

Are activities mandatory for residents?

Never. Participation is always optional, with gentle daily choices ranging from yoga and gardening to quiet reading.
